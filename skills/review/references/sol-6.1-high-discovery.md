# Sol 6.1 High discovery checklist

Trace each externally consumed value or decision in the changed runtime and directly affected call paths backward through its producers to supporting input or explicit product policy. Include values governing success, persistence and later processing. Identify the consumer, the value's intended meaning, its transformations, and reachable sources, defaults and fallbacks. Group paths only when their meaning, producers and consumers are equivalent.

Where the contract promises bounded work, resource use or processing only a prefix, inspect the observable work as well as the returned value. A correct result can still violate that contract. Report a supported efficiency defect at its demonstrated severity without assuming a production outage.

At consequential constants, bounds, substitutions, branch order or regex order, state the assumption that makes the choice valid. Check it against requirements, validated caller constraints or documented policy. Separate guaranteed inputs from properties merely observed in examples. An implementation default, constant or existing test does not independently establish the required policy. Distinguish absent, empty, invalid, unavailable and conflicting information where the contract does; check whether a downstream consumer receives justified information or an unsupported guess. Preserve documented defaults, permitted inference and valid partial results.

Before probing, make a compact coverage ledger for each changed decision: list its reachable alternatives, including no-match, single-match and multiple-match outcomes where applicable. Record existing test coverage and new review evidence separately. Do not count a failure in one alternative, or repeated examples of one cause, as evidence for its siblings. For decisions with overlapping matches, identify which alternatives can coexist and whether the grounded contract requires selection, combination or rejection; inspect the actual rule for each distinct reachable overlap. Prioritize alternatives with no tests or review evidence. Once a defect is established, move to an unexamined alternative before extending evidence for that defect, unless a concrete uncertainty affects its validity. Before finishing, reconcile the ledger with the findings and coverage statement. Keep blocked or unexamined alternatives visible within existing process limits. Where an alternative lacks a grounded expectation, record the missing contract without reporting a defect.

For every operation that selects one result from several matches, check zero, one and multiple eligible matches within the same logical input unit, as well as across units. Give coexisting matches distinct semantic roles. From the consumer contract, establish which role should determine the result. Pair a control with a valid case that adds or reorders a competing match before and after the intended match. Inspect first-match, last-match and priority choices separately. A failure in a later selection stage does not cover this earlier choice. If the required role or precedence is unknown, record that limit.

Before observing results, derive small discriminating inputs from the supported domain. Pair an ordinary control with the smallest valid change that challenges an assumption or removes the information supporting a fallback. Include a positive control for explicitly permitted defaults. For overlapping branches or competing sources, establish the required selection before testing order. For bounds, establish why excluded inputs fall outside the caller's contract. If the domain, precedence or permitted result remains unspecified, preserve that uncertainty instead of inventing an expectation.

Use existing permitted tools for isolated probes where useful. Establish each suspected violation through its reachable trigger, independently grounded expectation, actual result and concrete consumer consequence. Distinguish executed evidence from source-only reasoning. Decide whether a defect is supported before assigning its priority, and retain confirmed violations while completing the remaining scoped inspection. Having enough evidence for an overall verdict is not a stopping condition. Existing access and process limits still apply; disclose blocked or unfinished coverage.

Before consolidating findings, revisit candidates set aside solely because they seemed unimportant. Restore supported violations; keep disproved or unresolved candidates out of the findings. Consolidate duplicate causes, then assign priority from demonstrated consequences and applicable review criteria. Report all distinct supported defects in the normal format without inflating severity or adding speculative, stylistic or unsupported policy claims. Preserve the normal coverage statement. Reporting no findings is valid.

Before delivering each finding, verify its factual qualifiers against the complete relevant source control flow, including logs and error handling. Remove unsupported qualifiers while retaining the supported defect. Distinguish observed behavior from assumptions.

Establish how each required output value becomes known. Follow missing-field and default paths through their consumers. Challenge eligibility thresholds against independently supported requirements. For stable eligible input, check whether normal later attempts or processing passes can make the promised progress. Treat an unspecified output, eligibility or progress contract as unresolved. Use existing process limits.
